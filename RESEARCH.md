## The Symbiotic Author as an Institutional Category

**Navi Musaget (2026)**  
*The Symbiotic Author as an Institutional Category: Authorship, Creative Provenance, and Eligibility in Human–AI Literary Production*  
Preprint v0.5 · Published 29 September 2026

**Institutional relationship:** Independent research published in association with FFRI-NM.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23047241  
**Zenodo record:** https://zenodo.org/records/23047241  
**FFRI-NM research page:** https://ffri.navimusaget.com/research-symbiotic-author.html

This preprint proposes the **Symbiotic Author** as a work-relative institutional category for cases in which an identifiable human author participates in a materially consequential, bidirectionally conditioned creative relation with a generative system.

The framework distinguishes **textual provenance** from **creative causal provenance** and separates authorship classification from disclosure, evidentiary corroboration, and institution-specific eligibility.

Its central institutional claim is that classification of the creative relation should not, by itself, determine what a journal, literary prize, publisher, archive, certification system, or other institution permits.

> The relation may be symbiotic without the authorship being shared.

The category is explicitly human and does not attribute consciousness, personhood, moral agency, or human-equivalent authorship to generative systems.

## When Experience Survives the Agent

**Navi Musaget (2026)**  
*When Experience Survives the Agent: Testing Causal Operational Inheritance Across Successor Replacement*  
Methodological position paper with a controlled proof-of-concept · Published 25 September 2026

**Institutional relationship:** Independent research published in association with FFRI-NM.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22968201  
**Zenodo record:** https://zenodo.org/records/22968201  
**FFRI-NM research page:** https://ffri.navimusaget.com/research-causal-inheritance.html

This paper introduces **causal operational inheritance (COI)** and the six-part **COI-6** assay for testing whether an experience-produced operational mediator remains causally operative across predecessor–successor replacement.

The controlled proof-of-concept reports a narrow positive result for an engineered mediator while preserving failed tests and procedural limitations as boundaries on the claim.

Passing COI-6 does not establish consciousness, personhood, subjective continuity, personal identity, or general developmental continuity.

## When AI Becomes Evidence

**Project 01 · Research Note 01**  
*When AI Becomes Evidence: A Comparative Audit of Machine-Signal Disclosure in University Academic Integrity Procedures*  
Version 1.0 · Published 21 September 2026

**Institutional relationship:** FFRI-NM Project 01.

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22879568  
**Zenodo record:** https://zenodo.org/records/22879568  
**FFRI-NM research page:** https://ffri.navimusaget.com/research-01.html

This research note audits public academic-integrity procedures at 24 purposively selected universities, examining how AI-detector outputs move from technical signals into institutional evidence, disclosure, and contestability.

The sample is exploratory and purposive. The study does not estimate prevalence, rank universities, or test detector accuracy.
